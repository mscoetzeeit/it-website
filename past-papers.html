Past Papers

A shared folder of past National Senior Certificate (NSC) Information Technology exam papers and their memoranda. Working through real papers under exam conditions is the single best way to prepare.

📁Open the Past Papers folder (Google Drive)

Opens in a new tab. The folder holds Paper 1 (practical) and Paper 2 (theory) papers together with their marking guidelines.

How to use past papers

Open or print a paper, set a timer, and complete it without looking at the memo. Only mark yourself afterwards. Match the verb in each question (state / explain / discuss) and give as many facts as there are marks — then use the marking guideline to see exactly where the marks are awarded.

Also useful

Try the topic-by-topic Exam Practice questions (each with a reveal-memo), and learn the definitions word-for-word from the IT Glossary.

These are official Department of Basic Education / provincial papers, shared for study purposes only. If a link ever stops working, please let your teacher know.
