Practice Exam Papers

Download full, CAPS-aligned practice examination papers — Paper 1 (Practical) and Paper 2 (Theory) — each with a marking guideline (memo). Practical papers come with all the data files you need.

Please read first

These are PRACTICE papers written by the school to prepare for the exam. They are NOT official DBE/NSC papers. They follow the CAPS structure and mark allocations.

  • Do Paper 1 on a computer using Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Access).
  • Answer the HTML question in a text editor such as Notepad++ — not in a word processor.
  • Extract the data-files ZIP before you start. Save regularly.
  • Papers and memos are in Word format (.docx) so teachers can adapt and print them.
