How to print

Open a booklet and use your browser's Print option (Ctrl+P). Choose A4, double-sided, and save paper by printing in black and white. You can print only some pages by entering a page range, for example the contents page and the chapter you are revising.

Looking for something to write in?

These booklets are for reading and revising. For pen-and-paper practice with questions to answer by hand, try the Think & Write Workbooks, one for every term. For definitions, use the IT Glossary.
